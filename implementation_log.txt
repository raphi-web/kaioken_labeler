# Implementation log

Rationale and measurements that do not belong in source comments. Newest
entry first.

## 2026-10-09 — auto-encoder embedding as the wand's third source

The labeling build grew a third magic-wand distance source, `embed`, from
`auto_encoder/embedding.onnx` (contract: `auto_encoder/ONNX.md`). The model
replaces the never-wired prithvi encoder folder, which was deleted; a backup
lives outside the repo.

### Design decisions

- **New source, not a replacement.** The wand already dispatched on
  `source` ("image" | "sam"); "embed" is a third arm of that switch, so every
  downstream invariant (additivity, per-sample budgets, negatives, protect,
  global mode) is inherited and re-tested rather than reimplemented.
- **Pixel resolution, no upsample.** The graph encodes fixed 128×128 patches
  of raw DN; `EmbedService` tiles, batches (≤64 patches/run) and stitches to
  (8, H, W) at one cell per pixel, with edge-padding for non-multiples of
  128. Unlike SAM's coarse feature grids, the click's own distance survives
  exactly (pinned by `test_the_embed_field_is_exact_at_the_click`).
- **Same field maths as SAM.** `Wand._cosine_field` was extracted from the
  SAM path: map pixel → grid (identity here), mean-centre + unit-normalise
  the stack, one tensordot per click. Centring is load-bearing: measured on
  00.tiff's labels, the intra/inter cosine-distance ratio improves from
  0.043 (raw) to 0.018 (centred).
- **Hard gate at 10 bands.** The graph's input is fixed `(B, 10, 128, 128)`
  int16 DN; projects are band-count-configurable, so `Api._embed_unavailable`
  refuses non-10-band projects with a message instead of an ORT shape error,
  and the UI button greys out (refetched per project, since the gate is
  project-shaped while SAM's model-file check is global).
- **Spectral stays the default** (user decision, confirmed by the bench below).
- **One tolerance across classes** for embed, same policy as SAM: per-class
  optima have not been measured, and inventing seeds would dress a guess up
  as evidence. Each embedding source remembers its own slider position
  (`samTolerance` / `embedTolerance` settings fields).

### Bench: `backend/bench_wand.py sources --images 20`

470 labeled regions across 20 project images, each source at its own best
tolerance (per-region oracle over its swept range):

| source | mean IoU | median | IoU ≥ 0.5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| spectral | 0.543 | 0.550 | 55.5% |
| embed    | 0.507 | 0.534 | 52.8% |
| sam      | 0.469 | 0.478 | 48.1% |

Complementarity vs spectral:

| pair | wins over spectral | correlation | per-region union |
|---|---|---|---|
| embed | 205/470 | 0.658 | 0.605 (+11%) |
| sam   | 173/470 | 0.518 | 0.606 (+12%) |

Reading: embed lands between spectral and SAM overall, but wins on 44% of
regions with a moderate correlation — it fails where spectral fails
differently, which is the only property that justifies a third source. On
the 3-region pilot run embed looked best of all three (0.744); the 470-region
run corrected that, and the tooltip copy was written from the big run.

Embed tolerance curve (mean IoU per tolerance, 470 regions):

```
0.02 0.272 | 0.04 0.319 | 0.06 0.327 | 0.08 0.328 <- peak
0.10 0.318 | 0.12 0.303 | 0.14 0.294 | 0.16 0.278
0.20 0.253 | 0.30 0.211 | 0.40 0.189
band within 10% of peak: 0.04..0.12
```

→ `EMBED_TOL_FLOOR = 0.02`, `EMBED_TOL_CEIL = 0.14`, slider 50 = 0.08 =
peak, so the useful band spans slider 14..71 and the default sits on the low
side of the decay, matching the spectral wand's "over-selection costs more
than under-selection" policy.

### Encoding cost

One 512×512 tile = 16 patches = one batched run ≈ 0.09 s on CPU (Ryzen 5
PRO 8540U), vs seconds for a SAM2 encode. The first embed click on an image
pays it; the result is cached per image in `Wand._stacks["embed"]` and in
`EmbedService`, keyed so display-band changes do not re-encode (the encoder
consumes all 10 bands regardless).
